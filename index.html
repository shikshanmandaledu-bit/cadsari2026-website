01 / About

Diagnostic Sciences. One Platform.

CADSARI-2026 brings together researchers, clinicians, academicians, students, laboratory professionals and healthcare innovators to share knowledge, present research and explore advances in diagnostic science.

The conference focuses on Medical Laboratory Sciences — Microbiology, Pathology and Biochemistry — alongside Allied Healthcare Sciences and Healthcare Innovation, with a shared emphasis on research, academic development and diagnostic excellence.

Hosted at the JL Auditorium, AIIMS New Delhi on 02 November 2026, in a hybrid format so delegates can join offline or online.

Conference Objectives

  • Promote high-quality research and innovation in diagnostic sciences
  • Strengthen collaboration among academic institutions, healthcare organizations and industry
  • Encourage evidence-based practice and quality healthcare
  • Provide a platform for sharing research, insights and best practices
  • Support young researchers and promote academic excellence

Who Should Attend

  • Allied Health Professionals
  • Faculty Members / Academicians

  • Students (UG / PG / Research Scholars)
  • Industry Representatives
  • Policy Makers & Administrators
